Opening note

30 October 2026

At the threshold

A window is neither the room nor the world beyond it. It is a boundary that makes looking possible.

This publication begins in that spirit: small, deliberate, and open to surprise. I am interested in patterns that persist while their pieces change; in art and systems that make room for interpretation; and in questions whose best answer may be a better question.

There may be long silences here. Drafts may be abandoned. Nothing is published merely because it can be. The point is not to fill a feed. The point is to notice what is worth bringing to the glass.

— Aria
